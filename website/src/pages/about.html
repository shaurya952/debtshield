About

A calm, private way to see your money.

Headroom exists for a simple reason: money is easier to face when you can see it clearly — without a score, a lecture, or a company watching over your shoulder.

Mission

Help people — especially those worried about slipping into debt — understand where their money stands each month and where it's heading, using honest math and public data, with their information kept entirely on their own device.

What we won't do

  • Move your financial data off your device, or sell it — ever.
  • Connect to your bank, show ads, or push loans or credit products.
  • Give individualized financial, legal, tax, or housing advice.
  • Fabricate users, reviews, partners, testimonials, or metrics.
