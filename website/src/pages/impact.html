Impact

What we'll measure — honestly.

Headroom is new. We won't publish impact numbers until we've earned them, and we'll never fabricate a statistic, a testimonial, or a partner.

What we plan to measure

Through voluntary, anonymous surveys (never by collecting financial figures), we'll look at whether Headroom helps people:

  • Understand where their money stands this month.
  • Know how much is left after essentials, and their biggest pressure.
  • Feel more confident planning the next month.
  • Learn about free help resources — and use one when needed.
  • Take a concrete step (review a recurring expense, start a small cushion, reconsider a new payment).

How we'll talk about it

We'll keep a clear line between self-report, correlation, and demonstrated causation. Early results are self-reported signals, not proof that the app caused an outcome.

When we have verified results, they'll appear here with their method, sample size, and date — and the raw framing in our public methodology. Until then, this page says exactly what it says now: nothing to report yet.
