Privacy

Your numbers stay on your phone.

This is the whole point of Headroom. The app has no server for your financial data, no account, and no tracking — and it works fully offline.

In the app

  • Income, rent, debt, expenses, verdicts, and simulation results are stored only on your device.
  • No account and no sign-in — the app has no concept of a user identity.
  • No bank connection, no ads, no analytics, no advertising identifier.
  • Comparison data (Census, EIA, BLS) is bundled inside the app and read-only.
  • You can delete everything you entered in one tap — the app and its comparison data remain.

Because nothing is transmitted, there is nothing to breach on a server, nothing to sell, and nothing collected from anyone — including children.

On this website

This marketing website is separate from the app's private storage. The site itself sets no tracking cookies. If you choose to submit a form here — for example, a pilot inquiry — you send us the contact details you type into that form, and nothing else. The website never receives anything from the app.

Don't just take our word for it

You can use the app in Airplane Mode and it works the same. The app's privacy behavior is described in-app in the Trust Center, and the code is structured so financial data has no path off the device.
