Accessibility

Built to work for everyone.

Accessibility is a non-negotiable in DebtShield, not an afterthought. Here's what that means and how to tell us when we fall short.

Our commitments

  • Full Dynamic Type, up to the largest accessibility text sizes.
  • VoiceOver labels and a sensible reading order, with spoken summaries for charts.
  • WCAG-AA contrast in light and dark modes.
  • Color is never the only signal — every status also has words and an icon.
  • Reduce Motion is respected throughout.
  • Comfortable tap targets (at least 44×44 points).

This website

This site uses semantic HTML, a skip-to-content link, visible keyboard focus, strong contrast in light and dark, and no motion that ignores your “reduce motion” setting. It has no invasive tracking and no dark patterns.

Found a barrier?

Accessibility problems are bugs, and we want to fix them. In the app, use About ▸ Send feedback (choose “Accessibility issue”). A public contact address will be listed here once it's live.
