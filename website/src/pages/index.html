Common questions

Is it really private?

Yes. The numbers you enter are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. There's no server for your financial data, no analytics, and no tracking — it works fully offline, even in Airplane Mode. More on privacy.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No — and you can't. DebtShield never links to a bank or credit account. You type a few numbers yourself, which keeps it private and simple.

How much does it cost?

It's designed to be free for the people who use it — no ads, no subscriptions, and nothing to buy inside the app. Organizations can sponsor access for the people they serve.

Where do the comparison numbers come from?

Public U.S. data, bundled inside the app: rent and income from the Census Bureau, energy from the EIA, and food from the BLS. They're broad averages for perspective, never a target. See the sources.

Does the “Ask” feature use AI that sees my data?

No. Ask is deterministic — it computes every figure from your own numbers on your device and never sends anything to a server or guesses. It won't invent numbers, and it points you to free help for anything that needs a real person.

Is this financial advice?

No. DebtShield is educational information about your own month, with cited public data — not financial, legal, tax, or housing advice, and it doesn't promise any outcome. For decisions, it points you to free, reputable help like 211 and HUD-approved counselors.
