The privacy advantage

An institution never receives individual financial entries or individual DebtShield verdicts. Participants' numbers stay on their own phones. Any reporting is aggregate and voluntary — and only where it's ethically and technically valid.

  • No individual income, expenses, debt, verdicts, or simulation results.
  • No identifiable financial profiles shared with sponsors.
