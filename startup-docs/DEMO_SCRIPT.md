# Demo Script (~5 minutes)

_A calm, honest walkthrough. Use seeded, non-identifying numbers. Lead with the
problem and the privacy promise; don't oversell._

## 0. Framing (30s)
"DebtShield answers one question — how does my money stand this month, and where
is it heading — privately. No account, no bank connection; everything stays on
the phone."

## 1. Home / Safe Line (60s)
Show the hero: money left, the Safe Line bar, the one-line read. "Income is the
bar; essentials fill it; what's left is your room. No score, no judgment."

## 2. The verdict (45s)
Tap the verdict banner. "A plain read of the month, with the actual arithmetic
behind it. It weighs the dollars left, not just a ratio."

## 3. The year ahead (60s)
Open the tile. "We play the numbers forward — hundreds of runs — to show the
odds of dipping into the red, a likely range, and the single change that helps
most. It shows its assumptions; it's a projection, not a promise."

## 4. Compare (30s)
"How your rent, food, energy line up with your county and the U.S., from public
Census, EIA, and BLS data. Context, never a target."

## 5. Trust & privacy (45s)
Open About ▸ Trust Center. Show "what it never does". Then About ▸ "Delete my
numbers": "One tap wipes your entries; the app and its comparison data stay.
Institutions never see any of this."

## 6. Close (20s)
"Private by design, honest by design, and accessible by design. For
organizations, participants get this without you ever seeing their numbers."

## Reminders
- Turn on airplane mode at some point to show it works offline.
- Never show real personal finances. Never claim guaranteed outcomes.
