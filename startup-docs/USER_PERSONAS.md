# User Personas

_Illustrative personas to guide product and messaging. These are composite
archetypes, not real people, and carry no fabricated statistics._

## 1. "First-month" student — Maya, 19
- First-gen college student, part-time job, first time managing rent + bills.
- Anxious about money; embarrassed by "budgeting" apps that feel like homework.
- Won't link a bank account she barely trusts herself.
- **Needs:** a calm, private, plain-dollars picture; no jargon, no judgment.
- **Wins with:** Safe Line + verdict; "what changed" each month; free-help links.

## 2. "Tight but proud" young adult — Devon, 26
- Full-time but stretched; rent is high for the area; some card debt.
- Worried about slipping further; skeptical of anything that upsells loans.
- **Needs:** honest read on debt load; the one lever that helps most; privacy.
- **Wins with:** dollar-aware verdict; year-ahead odds + biggest lever; Compare.

## 3. "Thinking about a move" — Priya, 30
- Considering relocating for a job; unsure if she can afford a new city.
- **Needs:** a realistic cost read before committing.
- **Wins with:** "Could you afford to move here?" + local rent/energy data.

## 4. Program facilitator — Marcus (institutional buyer)
- Runs a community financial-wellness program; cares about participant trust and
  data liability; small budget; needs to show his org it helped.
- **Needs:** a private tool he can recommend; aggregate, voluntary outcomes;
  simple deployment + facilitator materials.
- **Wins with:** the privacy story + pilot proposal + aggregate reporting.

## Accessibility note
Assume every persona may use large text, VoiceOver, or Reduce Motion. The
product must work for them by default (see `ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md`).
