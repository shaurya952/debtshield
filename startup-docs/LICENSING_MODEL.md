# Licensing Model

_How institutions license DebtShield. Payments are **not** activated yet
(prepare, don't charge). We license sponsored access and services — **never**
personal data._

## What is licensed
- **Sponsored access** for an organization's participants (via TestFlight or the
  App Store), plus the **services** around it: deployment support, staff
  training, facilitator materials, annual data updates, accessibility docs,
  priority support, and **aggregate, voluntary** outcome reporting.

## What is NOT licensed or sold
- Individual financial data, verdicts, or simulation results — these never leave
  participants' devices and are never available to us or to the institution.
- No ads, no loan/credit referrals, no data brokering.

## Structures (choose per customer; prices are placeholders to validate)
1. **Time-boxed pilot** — flat fee (or waived for a first partner in exchange for
   feedback), fixed term, defined deliverables.
2. **Annual program license** — tiered by organization size / participant band;
   includes updates + support.
3. **Grant / sponsorship** — a funder underwrites access for a population.

## Term & renewal
- Fixed term (pilot) or annual (license), with a renewal conversation before
  expiry. No auto-renew traps; clear cancellation.

## Included vs add-on
- **Included:** the app, standard onboarding materials, email support.
- **Add-on:** custom training, co-branded educational pages, expanded aggregate
  reporting, on-site support.

## Guardrails
- The privacy boundary (`CUSTOMER_DATA_BOUNDARIES.md`) is non-negotiable and
  appears in every agreement.
- Legal terms are **drafts requiring attorney review**
  (`SAMPLE_PILOT_AGREEMENT_REQUIREMENTS.md`).
- Pricing is validated through pilots (`PRICING_TEST_PLAN.md`), never fabricated.
