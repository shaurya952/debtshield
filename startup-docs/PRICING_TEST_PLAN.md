# Pricing Test Plan

_The operational plan for validating price during real pilots. Hypotheses live in
`PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md`; this is how we test them without fabricating demand._

## Goal
Find a price that mission-driven buyers will actually pay for the **service**,
that is fair to nonprofits, and that doesn't imply data monetization.

## Sequence
1. **Discovery pricing questions** (before quoting): budget cycle, who signs,
   what a "yes" would require. Van Westendorp: at what price is it *too cheap to
   trust*, *a bargain*, *getting expensive*, *too expensive*.
2. **Anchored options** in the pilot proposal: present 2–3 named options (e.g.
   flat pilot fee vs annual license) and see which is chosen and where they balk.
3. **First-partner offer**: consider waiving/deep-discounting the very first
   pilot in exchange for candid feedback and (optional, only if they volunteer) a
   testimonial — never a paid-for review.
4. **Iterate**: adjust tiers/discounts based on real objections logged in
   `SALES_PIPELINE_SCHEMA.md`.

## What to measure (honest)
- Quote → close rate and time-to-close by option.
- Objection reasons (price vs privacy vs timing vs authority).
- Nonprofit vs employer willingness-to-pay.
- Renewal intent at end of pilot.
Record with source + date; never present interest as revenue
(`METRICS_DEFINITIONS.md`).

## Guardrails
- Prices in docs are **placeholders to validate**, not published rates.
- Never claim "others pay X" without a real, cited data point.
- Never tie price to access to individual data (there is none to sell).

## Decision criteria
- Proceed if ≥ [N] qualified buyers accept a pilot fee at a sustainable price.
- If mission buyers can't pay, pivot toward grants/sponsorship rather than
  compromising privacy or inflating claims.
