# Organization Requirements

_What an organization needs to run a DebtShield pilot or license — and what we
require of them. Deliberately light; the app is on-device, so there's little to
set up._

## What the organization needs
- Participants with **iPhones on iOS 17+** (the app is iOS-only today).
- A **staff facilitator** to introduce it and answer basic questions (we train
  them).
- A way to **distribute** the app: TestFlight (beta) or the App Store (launch),
  via a shared link or code.
- Willingness to honor the **privacy model**: they will not ask participants for
  their in-app numbers, and they accept aggregate-only reporting.

## What the organization does NOT need
- No servers, no IT integration, no SSO, no data pipeline.
- No collection or storage of participants' financial data (there is none to
  collect).
- No bank connections or account provisioning.

## What we provide
- Onboarding + facilitator materials, a short staff training, email support,
  and (optionally) a voluntary anonymous survey + aggregate report.

## What we require
- A named contact and agreement to the privacy boundaries
  (`CUSTOMER_DATA_BOUNDARIES.md`).
- Honest use: no implying DebtShield guarantees outcomes, and no pressuring
  participants to share private figures.
- For any survey: voluntary participation and the approved consent language.

## Accessibility & inclusion
- Facilitators should know the app supports large text, VoiceOver, and Reduce
  Motion, and should offer help to participants who need it.

## Support & escalation
- Standard: email support with a target response window (set at agreement time).
- Escalations (a wrong benchmark, an accessibility barrier) route to the same
  feedback + incident process (`INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PLAN.md`).
