## DebtShield — private money clarity for the people you serve

**The problem.** Money stress is high, and mainstream tools require bank
connections or push products — a privacy and trust barrier, especially for
students and community-program participants.

**DebtShield.** A calm, private iPhone app that shows how someone's money stands
each month, and where it's heading, in plain dollars — with no score, no
judgment, and no bank connection. Everything stays on the person's device.

**Why partners choose it**
- **Privacy without compromise** — you never see individual financial data;
  participants' numbers never leave their phones.
- **Trust** — no ads, no upsells, no loan referrals; free-help links (211, HUD).
- **Honest & accessible** — cited public data, transparent methods, WCAG-AA.

**How a pilot works**
1. Short staff training + facilitator materials.
2. Participants join via a simple link/code and use it for their own month.
3. Optional voluntary, anonymous survey → an aggregate end-of-pilot report.

**What you get**
Deployment support, training, materials, and aggregate (never individual)
outcomes.
