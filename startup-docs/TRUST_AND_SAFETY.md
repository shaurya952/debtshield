# Trust & Safety

_How DebtShield protects vulnerable users and stays honest. Money apps touch
people in distress; the bar is high._

## Core commitments
- **Educational only.** No individualized financial, legal, tax, credit,
  housing, or bankruptcy advice. Every surface says so.
- **No dark patterns.** No fake urgency, guilt, streaks, shame, or manipulative
  notifications. Reminders are opt-in and pressure-free.
- **No harmful monetization.** No ads, payday-loan/credit/debt-settlement
  referrals, or product upsells.
- **No false hope or false alarm.** Verdicts and odds are calibrated and shown
  with assumptions/ranges; we never claim to prevent debt or guarantee outcomes.

## Distress & crisis
- When users raise loans, bankruptcy, benefits, eviction, or serious hardship,
  the app **redirects to reputable free help** (211, HUD counselors,
  Benefits.gov) rather than advising.
- The app surfaces **988** (mental-health crisis) where appropriate and states
  it is **not** an emergency service.
- Ask is **deterministic** — it cannot hallucinate advice or numbers; it restates
  and computes from the user's own figures and bundled data only.

## Data & privacy safety
- Financial data stays on-device; nothing is transmitted. One-tap delete.
- Optional Face ID lock; background-snapshot masking.
- Website contact forms are separate, consented, and minimal (no financial data).

## Vulnerable users
- Plain, non-shaming language throughout; readable at large text and with
  VoiceOver.
- No age-gating needed because nothing is collected; not directed at children.

## Content review
- Any change to verdict wording, thresholds, or the year-ahead framing is
  reviewed for false precision, panic, or false reassurance before shipping
  (see `../RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` and `PUBLIC_CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`).
