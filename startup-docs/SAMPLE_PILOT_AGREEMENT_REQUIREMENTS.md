# Sample Pilot Agreement — Requirements (NOT a contract)

> **⚠️ This is a requirements checklist / term-sheet outline for discussion, not
> a legal agreement. It has not been reviewed by an attorney. Do not sign or send
> anything derived from this without professional legal review.**

_What a real pilot agreement must cover. Use it to brief counsel, not as the
contract itself._

## Parties & scope
- Legal names of DebtShield entity and the organization; authorized signers.
- Scope: number of participants, distribution method, term dates, deliverables
  (materials, training, optional survey + aggregate report).

## Fees & payment
- Fee (or waiver for first partner), invoicing, payment terms, taxes.
- No fees tied to individual data (there is none to sell).

## Privacy & data — the core
- Verbatim-in-spirit `CUSTOMER_DATA_BOUNDARIES.md`: no individual financial data
  or verdicts ever shared; participants' data stays on-device.
- Voluntary, anonymous surveys only; aggregate reporting with small-cell
  suppression; consent language; retention + deletion terms.
- Subprocessors disclosed; data-processing terms as applicable.

## Participant protection
- Voluntary participation; no coercion to share private figures.
- Accessibility supported; free-help resources surfaced (211/HUD/988) — the app
  is not an emergency service.

## Legal / risk
- **Educational disclaimer:** not financial, legal, tax, or housing advice; no
  guaranteed outcomes.
- Warranties limited; liability capped; "as is" where lawful.
- IP: DebtShield retains app/content IP; org retains its own marks; bundled
  public data (Census/EIA/BLS) is public domain.
- Compliance with applicable laws (incl. any education/consumer/privacy rules
  the org is subject to) — flag for counsel.

## Term, termination, changes
- Term dates; termination for convenience/cause; effect on data (delete survey
  data per policy).
- How changes/renewal are handled; no auto-renew traps.

## Signatures
- Authorized representatives; effective date.

## Reminder
Every bracket and clause above is a **prompt for counsel**, not final language.
The privacy boundary is non-negotiable regardless of how the contract is drafted.
