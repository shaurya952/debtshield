# Tester Recruitment Guide

_Recruit a small, relevant closed beta (aim: 20 engaged testers). Never
fabricate testers or their numbers._

## Who
Initial market: college students, young adults, first-gen students, community-
program participants, and anyone worried about monthly financial instability.
Aim for a mix of comfort levels with money and with iPhones, and include people
who use large text / VoiceOver.

## Where (honest, permission-based)
- Campus groups, financial-wellness offices, and student orgs (with staff OK).
- Community nonprofits and workforce programs already trusted by participants.
- Personal network — clearly labeled as a beta, no pressure.

## The ask (sample)
> I'm testing a private, on-device iPhone app that shows where your money stands
> each month — no account, no bank connection, nothing leaves your phone. Would
> you try it for a few weeks and tell me what's confusing? ~20 min to start,
> then a quick check-in.

## Consent & expectations
- It's a beta; things may change or break.
- No financial data is collected; feedback is voluntary and on their terms.
- They can leave any time and delete their data in one tap (About ▸ Delete my
  numbers).

## Do not
- Do not offer payment tied to a "positive review."
- Do not imply it prevents debt/eviction/bankruptcy.
- Do not collect their financial figures "to help" — you don't need them.
