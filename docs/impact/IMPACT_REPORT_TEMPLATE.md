**Interpretation (required).**
These are **self-reports** from people who chose to use the app. A pre→post
change is **correlation over time, not proof the app caused it** — there is no
control group and participants self-selected. We therefore say "participants
reported [X] changed," never "DebtShield improved [X]."

**Limitations.**
- Small / self-selected sample; expectancy and social-desirability effects.
- Self-report, not observed behavior; the app can't (by design) observe usage.
- Single-arm pre/post; no counterfactual.

**What we'd need for stronger evidence.**
A larger sample, a comparison group, and — if framed as research — proper ethics
oversight and pre-registration.
