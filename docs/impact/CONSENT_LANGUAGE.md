# Consent Language (voluntary survey)

_Shown before the pre-survey. Plain, honest, no pressure. This is a voluntary
product survey, **not** formal academic human-subjects research — do not frame it
as IRB-approved research unless appropriate oversight has actually been obtained._

---

**About this survey**

Thanks for helping improve DebtShield. This short survey asks how you think and
feel about your money and what, if anything, you did — not your actual numbers.

- **It's voluntary.** You can skip any question or stop at any time.
- **It's anonymous.** We don't ask for your name, email, or any financial
  figures. You'll create a simple code so we can match your before/after answers
  without knowing who you are.
- **What we do with it.** We combine everyone's answers to understand whether the
  app helps people feel clearer and more confident. We report only **aggregate**
  results, never individual responses.
- **It's separate from the app.** Your financial numbers stay on your phone and
  are never part of this survey.
- **Retention & deletion.** We keep responses only as long as needed for the
  study and then delete them. Because it's anonymous, tell us your code if you
  want a specific response removed. (See the data retention policy.)

There is no benefit or penalty either way, and nothing here is financial advice.

- [ ] I'm 18 or older and I agree to take this voluntary, anonymous survey.

---

_If this ever becomes formal research (e.g., for publication), obtain proper
ethics/IRB oversight and revise this language first._
