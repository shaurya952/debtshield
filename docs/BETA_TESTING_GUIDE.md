# Beta Testing Guide

_How the DebtShield closed beta runs. Goal: learn whether the app is clear,
trustworthy, and useful — without ever collecting anyone's financial data._

## What testers do
1. Install via TestFlight (`[HUMAN]` — needs an Apple Developer Program account
   and a build uploaded).
2. Use the app for their own real month: add numbers, read the verdict, open the
   year-ahead, compare, try "Save & earn more".
3. Come back next month and use "update this month → see what changed".
4. Send feedback from **About ▸ Send feedback** (or Trust Center ▸ Corrections).

## What we ask them to look for
- Was anything **confusing** or **scary**? Where did you hesitate?
- Did any number look **wrong**?
- Did it feel **judgmental** anywhere?
- Anything hard to read / tap / use with VoiceOver or large text?

## What we never collect
No income, rent, debt, verdicts, county, or simulation results. Feedback is
assembled on-device and the tester chooses what to include (see
`BETA_FEEDBACK_SCHEMA.md`). We do not run analytics during the beta.

## Cadence
- Week 0: onboarding + first-month use.
- Week 1: short check-in (interview or async).
- Week 4–5: second-month use — the key retention signal.
- End: optional short survey (Phase 11 protocol) + a thank-you.

## Success signals (self-reported, honest)
- Testers can state, in their words, where their money stands this month.
- They completed a **second month**.
- They found at least one thing useful; they trust it with their numbers.
