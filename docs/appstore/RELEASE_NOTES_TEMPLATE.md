# Release Notes Template ("What's New")

_Plain, honest, user-facing. No hype, no fake urgency. ≤ 4,000 chars._

## Template
```
What's new in <version>:

• <Short, concrete change in plain words>
• <Another change — a fix, a clarity improvement, an option>

Your numbers still stay on your phone. Questions or a wrong benchmark? Send
feedback from About ▸ Send feedback.
```

## v1.0 (first release) — draft
```
Welcome to DebtShield — a private, on-device way to see where your money stands.

• Safe Line: your income, what essentials take, and what's left — in plain dollars.
• The year ahead: a transparent look at your odds, with its assumptions shown.
• Compare: how your costs line up with your area and the U.S., using public data.
• Ask: answers from your own numbers, plus links to free help when you need it.
• Private by design: no account, no bank connection, no tracking. Delete
  everything in one tap.

DebtShield is educational information, not financial advice.
```

## Guidance
- Keep the privacy reminder in every set of notes.
- Never imply guaranteed outcomes.
- If a change affects methodology or a threshold, say so plainly and point to the
  in-app Trust Center.
- Match the `MARKETING_VERSION`; bump the build for every upload.
