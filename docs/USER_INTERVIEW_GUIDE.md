# User Interview Guide

_A short, non-leading interview (~20 min). Learn how people think about their
money and where the app helps or confuses. Never ask for their actual figures._

## Ground rules
- The app, not the person, is being tested. There are no wrong answers.
- Don't ask for real dollar amounts. Ask about understanding and feelings.
- Ask open questions; let silences sit. Don't sell.

## Warm-up
- How do you usually keep track of money month to month?
- What's stressful about it, if anything?

## First run (watch them, mostly quiet)
- "Set it up as you normally would." Note where they pause or look unsure.
- After the home screen: "In your own words, what is this telling you?"
- "Is anything here confusing, surprising, or off-putting?"

## Specific screens
- Verdict: "What does this say about your month? How does the wording feel?"
- The year ahead: "What do you think this number means? Do you trust it? Why?"
- Compare: "Is this useful? Does it feel like a target or just context?"

## Trust & privacy
- "Where do you think your numbers go?" (Listen for whether the on-device model
  landed.)
- "Would you be comfortable putting your real numbers in? What would make you
  more comfortable?"

## Wrap
- "One thing you'd change first?"
- "Would you use this again next month? Why / why not?"

## After
Write notes without any of their financial figures. Capture quotes about
clarity, trust, and usefulness — those drive the roadmap.
